# Set up Feed Block on your child’s Android phone

There are two permissions you might be asked about. The first lets you install Feed Block from a downloaded file (the APK). The second lets Feed Block recognise Spotlight, Reels or Shorts when your child opens them.

## Install the app

Download the signed APK on your child’s phone, open it and tap **Install**. If it installs straight away, skip to **Turn on Accessibility** below. [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/xevxx/feed-block-android/releases)

If the installation stops, follow the message you see. A supervised Samsung phone can show more than one of these prompts.

- **Family Link**. On your phone, open **Family Link** and select the child. Go to **Controls → Signed-in devices** (or **Supervised devices**), choose the child’s Android phone, and turn on **Apps from unknown sources** for the installation. [Google Family Link device controls](https://support.google.com/families/answer/10436839) · [Google’s unknown-source setting](https://support.google.com/families/answer/18753591)
- **Samsung Galaxy**. If **Auto Blocker** prevents installation, go to **Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker** on the Galaxy and turn it off temporarily. [Samsung Auto Blocker guide](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/mobile-devices/protect-your-galaxy-device-with-the-new-auto-blocker-feature/)
- **Allow from this source**. If Android asks for installation permission, open **Settings** from the prompt and turn on **Allow from this source** for the app opening the APK, such as your browser or file manager. You can also search Android Settings for **Install unknown apps**. Return to the APK and tap **Install**. [Google’s APK installation guide](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/7391672) · [Samsung install permissions guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/troubleshoot/TSG10001913/)

Once Feed Block is installed, turn Auto Blocker back on if you changed it. Put the Family Link and **Install unknown apps** settings back as you found them.

## Turn on Accessibility

1. Open Feed Block and tap **Open Accessibility settings**. Look for **Feed Block** in the list of installed or downloaded services. On a Samsung Galaxy, look under **Installed apps**. [Samsung Accessibility guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001906/)
2. Select **Feed Block**, turn it on and confirm Android’s prompt. Feed Block uses this access to spot familiar controls, such as the Shorts player. It checks them on the phone and has no internet permission.
3. If Android says **Restricted setting** or the switch is greyed out, go to **Settings → Apps → Feed Block** (tap **See all apps** if needed). On Feed Block’s **App info** page, tap the **three dots (⋮)** at the top and choose **Allow restricted settings**. Confirm, then return to Accessibility and turn on Feed Block. This extra step can appear on Android 13 and later, including phones from other manufacturers. [Google’s restricted settings guide](https://support.google.com/android/answer/12623953)

## Choose a feed and try it together

Back in Feed Block, turn on a switch under **Feed blocking** and set your six-digit parent PIN when asked. Each switch works independently.

Try a pair that fits your family. Open Snapchat Spotlight, then go back to a chat. Play a video from Instagram Home, then swipe to the next Reel. Or open a YouTube Short, then try a regular video. You should see the block screen on the feed you chose and be able to carry on with the chat or video you picked.

If you also want a PIN prompt on Feed Block’s own Android settings pages, tap **Protect Feed Block settings**. Coverage depends on the phone. [Parent controls and compatibility](../README.md#what-can-my-child-change)
